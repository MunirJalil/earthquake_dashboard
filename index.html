Colombia: Earthquake Macroeconomic Impact Simulator

Macro & Strategy · Andean Region · interactive scenario tool (semi-structural QPM)
Scenario illustrations · not forecasts · anchored to BTG Public Finance damage assessment

How to read this

Each panel shows the earthquake's effect on a macro variable through 2030, generated by our semi-structural Quarterly Projection Model. Drag Capital damage to scale the size of the capital-stock destruction (as a share of GDP); drag the overlay to scale the accompanying food/regulated cost-push and risk-premium shocks (100% = the calibration used in our research note). The presets follow our Public Finance desk's physical-damage assessment: Low 0.7%, Central 1.3% and High 2.2% of GDP. Because the model is linear, every path is an exact combination of these two channels, so the tool recomputes them live with no re-simulation needed.

Caveats

These are scenario illustrations, not forecasts. The "Impact" view is the deviation from the no-quake baseline (invariant to the baseline, given linearity); the "Levels" view adds that deviation to the model's own quarterly baseline. The damage sizing follows our Public Finance desk's preliminary in-house assessment (central physical damage of 1.3% of GDP, within a 0.7% to 2.2% range skewed to the upside); that desk separately estimates the net fiscal cost at about 0.8% of GDP. The overlay bundles the cost-push and risk-premium channels; the calibration is illustrative and to be refined against the 1999 Armenia benchmark and firming official data. Real GDP and the real exchange rate are in 100·log units (changes are approximately percent); a higher real exchange rate denotes real depreciation. Source: BTG Macro & Strategy semi-structural model; BTG Pactual Public Finance; DANE; Banco de la República; USGS.
